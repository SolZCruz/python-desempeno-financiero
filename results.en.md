# Report: Financial Performance of the 1,000 Largest Companies in Colombia

**Author:** Sol Zharick Cruz Ariza
**Period analyzed:** 2017 – 2018
**Source:** [Open Data Colombia — 1,000 Largest Companies in the Country](https://www.datos.gov.co/Econom-a-y-Finanzas/1000-Empresas-mas-grandes-del-pa-s/8hn7-rpp8)

---

## Analysis question

Which macro-sector had the best financial performance between 2017 and 2018, and which region concentrates that performance? As a closing step, how concentrated is that performance in a few companies?

---

## 1. Performance by macro-sector

![Growth by macro-sector](figures/01_crecimiento_por_macrosector.png)

The **Mining-Hydrocarbons** macro-sector led growth between 2017 and 2018, with a **25.35% increase in revenue** and **80.91% in profit** — well above Construction (20.98% / 31.87%) and Services (16.62% / 74.45%). Manufacturing and Agriculture and Livestock, despite having more companies, showed the lowest relative growth (7.66% and 9.01% respectively).

![Boxplot of revenue by macro-sector](figures/03_boxplot_ingresos_macrosector.png)

This boxplot gives context to the previous finding: Mining-Hydrocarbons also shows high dispersion in revenue, meaning that very large companies coexist with much smaller ones within the same sector — an early sign of the concentration that is confirmed later.

## 2. Geographic concentration

![Regional distribution](figures/02_distribucion_regional.png)

Within the leading macro-sector, **Bogotá-Cundinamarca concentrates 43 of the 54 companies** and more than 90% of the sector's revenue — far above the Atlantic Coast (7 companies) and Antioquia (3 companies). The leadership of the mining-energy sector is not geographically distributed; it depends almost entirely on a single region.

## 3. Who are the leading companies?

![Top 10 companies](figures/04_top_empresas.png)

Ecopetrol dominates the group by a wide margin, followed by Drummond and Carbones del Cerrejón. This confirms that the sector's growth is not a widespread phenomenon among many mid-sized companies, but is driven by a handful of large-scale companies, most of them in the hydrocarbons and coal sector.

## 4. Revenue vs. profitability

![Revenue vs. profit](figures/05_ingresos_vs_ganancia.png)

Not all companies with higher revenue are the most profitable — there is considerable dispersion in the revenue/profit relationship within the leading group, which suggests that size alone does not guarantee a better profit margin.

## 5. Revenue concentration (closing)

![Concentration curve](figures/06_curva_concentracion.png)

The most striking finding of the analysis: within the leading group (Mining-Hydrocarbons in Bogotá-Cundinamarca), the **top 10% of companies (4 of 43) generate 72.95% of revenue**, and the top 20% (9 companies) generate **83.47%**. The curve departs strongly from the line of equal distribution, confirming a very high concentration.

---

## Overall conclusion

The best financial performance in the country between 2017 and 2018 was concentrated in the Mining-Hydrocarbons macro-sector, led almost entirely by companies located in Bogotá-Cundinamarca. However, that leadership does not reflect a healthy, widely distributed sector: it depends on a very small number of large-scale companies (with Ecopetrol at the head), which represents a concentration risk — if any of these few companies runs into difficulties, the impact on the sector's aggregate figures would be significant.

## Limitations

- The analysis uses non-consolidated financial data (individual by company), which may underestimate the real weight of business groups with multiple subsidiaries.
- It only covers 2017-2018; without data from additional years, it is not possible to determine whether the concentration is structural or cyclical.
- It does not incorporate external macroeconomic variables (oil price, exchange rate) that probably explain a large part of the growth of the mining-energy sector in that period.
